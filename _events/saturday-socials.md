Saturday social swing dance party at Black Pepper Swing!

Doors open at 19:00. Entrance fee 10€. Door code {{ site.door_code }}.

DJs play their curated selection of music for swing dancers. Our mini-bar has low- and non-alc party drinks like beers, ciders, and soda pops. The floor eagerly welcomes all the swing dancers and swing dance curious.

Tickets available at the door.

Doors open at 19:00  
Doors close at 23:00


**General program**

Doors open at 19:00 with smooth jazz, tea, and coffee. The dance floor officially opens at 19:30 when we shift gears to the curated selection of music for swing dancers. The party continues until 23:00.
